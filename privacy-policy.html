Privacy Policy – Bumpi GO

Effective date: 14 November 2026
Developer: Spica Studio ("we", "us", "our")
Contact: spicastudio1@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game Bumpi GO ("the Game") uses and how it is handled. We wrote it to be short and clear, because the Game is simple in this respect: the Game does not collect your personal information and does not send any data about you to us or to anybody else.

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account to play. We never ask for your email address, phone number, real name, address or any ID.
  • The Game works fully offline. It does not use the internet to play.
  • Everything the Game remembers (your progress and your choices) is stored only on your own device.
  • The Game contains no analytics, no tracking and no advertising in this version.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you stopped, the Game saves the following locally on your device (in the game's normal save storage; on Android in the app's private storage, on Windows in the current user's registry under the game's name):

  • the player name you typed and the picture and country (flag) you chose;
  • your progress: coins, gems, lives, unlocked stages, owned cars, upgrades and paints, mission and daily reward progress, results such as wins, races and distance driven;
  • your settings: sound, graphics and battery options, steering layout, control size, opacity and positions;
  • the date and time of your last daily reward, so that rewards can reset each day.

This information never leaves your device through the Game. We cannot see it. The player name and country are shown only inside the Game (for example on your profile and in the Challenge lobby) and are not shared with other players or servers.

3. Information we do not collect

The Game does not collect, access or request: your location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, phone number, email address, advertising identifier, device identifiers, or any health or financial information. The Android app lists the standard Internet permission (the game engine adds it by default), but the Game's own code does not use the network and sends no data; the engine's analytics, advertising and crash-reporting services are switched off in this version.

4. Challenge mode

In Challenge mode the opponents (names, countries and pictures) are simulated by the Game on your device. They are not real people, and no multiplayer server is used. Nothing about you is sent to anyone.

5. Purchases and rewarded videos

The current version of the Game contains no in-app purchases and no advertising. Coins, lives, cars, upgrades and paints are earned only by playing, and nothing in the Game can be bought with real money.

If a future version adds real in-app purchases or advertising, it will use the services of the platform (for example Google Play Billing) and an advertising provider. Those services may process data such as an advertising ID, device information and purchase information under their own privacy policies. Before that happens we will update this policy and, where the law requires, ask for your consent.

6. Links that open outside the Game

The "Terms" and "Privacy Policy" buttons in the Game open these documents in your web browser (they are hosted on GitHub Pages). When you open a link, the website you visit receives your usual browser information and handles it under its own privacy policy. The Game itself does not send anything.

7. Children

The Game is meant for a general audience. Because the Game does not collect personal information from anyone, it also does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please write to us at spicastudio1@gmail.com.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

Your saved data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Bumpi GO → Storage → Clear data (or uninstall the Game).
  • Windows: uninstall or delete the Game; the saved values are stored in the registry under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\BumpiGO\Bumpi GO and can be deleted there.

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete, export or correct on our side. You can still contact us with any request.

9. Security

Your saved data is kept in the Game's normal private storage on your device. No data is transmitted by the Game, which removes the risks that come with sending data over a network. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please protect your device as you normally would.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal information, or to object to its processing. As explained above, we do not collect or hold personal information about you. If you believe we do, please contact us and we will respond.
