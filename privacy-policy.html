Privacy Policy – Bumpi GO

Effective date: 14 November 2026
Developer: Spica Studio ("we", "us", "our")
Contact: spicastudio1@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game Bumpi GO ("the Game") uses and how it is handled. We wrote it to be short and clear, because the Game is simple in this respect: the Game does not collect your personal information, and the only data that leaves your device is what the purchase service and the advertising service need (section 5).

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account to play. We never ask for your email address, phone number, real name, address or any ID.
  • You can play fully offline. The Game uses the internet only when you open the store, buy something or watch an optional ad (section 5).
  • Everything the Game remembers (your progress and your choices) is stored on your own device. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat (section 5).
  • The only advertising is an optional rewarded video that you choose to watch (for example to get a life). There are no banners and no ads in the middle of a race. The Game has no analytics of its own.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you stopped, the Game saves the following locally on your device (in the game's normal save storage; on Android in the app's private storage, on Windows in the current user's registry under the game's name):

  • the player name you typed and the picture and country (flag) you chose;
  • your progress: coins, gems, lives, unlocked stages, owned cars, upgrades and paints, mission and daily reward progress, results such as wins, races and distance driven;
  • your settings: sound, graphics and battery options, steering layout, control size, opacity and positions;
  • the date and time of your last daily reward, so that rewards can reset each day.

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it. The player name and country are shown only inside the Game (for example on your profile and in the Challenge lobby) and are not shared with other players or servers.

3. Information we do not collect

The Game does not collect, access or request: your exact location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, phone number, email address, or any health or financial information. The Android app uses the Internet permission only for in-app purchases and for the optional ads (section 5). The game engine's analytics and crash-reporting services are switched off.

4. Challenge mode

In Challenge mode the opponents (names, countries and pictures) are simulated by the Game on your device. They are not real people, and no multiplayer server is used. Nothing about you is sent to anyone.

5. In-app purchases and advertising

Coins, cars, upgrades and paints can be earned by playing. Some items can also be bought with real money (coin and gem packs, premium cars and special paints), and you can watch an optional rewarded video ad to get a life, double the coins of a race or open a bonus box.

Purchases

  • Payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details.
  • Delivering and restoring purchases: we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to confirm purchases with Google Play and to give you your items again after a reinstall or on a new phone. When you open the store or buy something, RevenueCat receives the product you bought, the purchase (transaction) ID, an anonymous app user ID that RevenueCat generates (not your name or email), and the technical information that comes with every internet request, such as the app version, your device's operating system, country and language, and your IP address. We do not receive this as personal data about you; we only see that a purchase was valid.
  • This information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and is handled under the privacy policies of Google Play and RevenueCat.

Advertising

  • The ads are shown by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To load and show an ad, AdMob and its partners may receive your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location that can be derived from it), device and app information, and how you interacted with the ad. This is handled under Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the Game first shows a consent form from Google, and you can change your choice later. Where you do not agree, ads that are not personalised may still be shown, or no ad is shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads).
  • We do not show ads to children under 13 on purpose: the Game is meant for players aged 13 and over.

If a future version adds other services, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires, ask for your consent.

6. Links that open outside the Game

The "Terms" and "Privacy Policy" buttons in the Game open these documents in your web browser (they are hosted on GitHub Pages). When you open a link, the website you visit receives your usual browser information and handles it under its own privacy policy.

7. Children

The Game is meant for a general audience. Because the Game does not collect personal information from anyone, it also does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please write to us at spicastudio1@gmail.com.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

Your saved data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Bumpi GO → Storage → Clear data (or uninstall the Game).
  • Windows: uninstall or delete the Game; the saved values are stored in the registry under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\BumpiGO\Bumpi GO and can be deleted there.

We do not hold your saved data. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat, and advertising data by Google; to ask for the purchase information that RevenueCat holds to be deleted, write to us at spicastudio1@gmail.com with the date of the purchase and we will pass the request on. You can contact us with any other request too.

9. Security

Your saved data is kept in the Game's normal private storage on your device. The only data the Game sends over the internet is the purchase and advertising information described in section 5. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please protect your device and your Google account as you normally would.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal information, or to object to its processing. As explained above, we do not collect or hold personal information about you; the purchase and advertising information in section 5 is processed by Google Play, RevenueCat and Google AdMob. If you believe we hold information about you, please contact us and we will respond.
